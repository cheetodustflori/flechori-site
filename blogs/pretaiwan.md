This past Sunday, Jina, Ellen, and Kou left for church without me. Normally, James drives all of us using the van, but Jina drove her car and abandoned Kay and me without a word >:(

**To say I was upset was an understatement** 😡

I called Kou while I was in the van and was grilling her and they just pretended I was cutting out 😭 what the heckkk


But then at church.... I realized... they wanted to surprise me with a Jellycat cake.... 🍰 teehee oopsie. Kay pulled out a poster (that looked like a funeral poster) and had everyone at church sign it for me. 

Honestly, this surprise was so incredibly bittersweet. I was overwhelmed with so much love and compassion... and now I have to leave it all for an country where no one will truly know or care for me like my friends currently do. I wanted to feel more fully grateful, but I suddenly had this nagging sense of doubt. 

*Am I making the right decision to leave?* 
*I spent so long developing these precious relationships - why am I putting it on pause?* 

This post is my reflection on **sisterhood, community in faith, and the little that I've learned and more I want to learn about having a steadfast faith** through *all situations.*

## Sisterhood

I've been blessed beyond words. I have parents who have instilled so many valuable lessons in me about relationships: give more than you expect back, be patient and understanding, and don't judge with a standard that you wouldn't apply to yourself. They've poured out so much love and effort into teaching me and I have no doubt these lessons have contributed to the solid foundation of friendships I have now.

In college, I have developed some incredible relationships. I have sisters who amaze me everyday with their selflessness, humility, tenacity, desire to include everyone, love for serving, and genuine kindness. 

We also fight. We hurt each other. We act pridefully and selfishly as well. We cast unrighteous judgment upon each other. We are so obviously sinners in need of saving -- I'm glad we can walk through these struggles together. What a blessing. 

Thank God for how He's placed these girls in my life. If I could walk through each person, you would also clearly see His work by how all of our paths crossed unexpectedly.

Lately, I've been confronted with some hard truths as well.
