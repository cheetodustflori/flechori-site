## Hello, NTU!

My first week of school! Let's gooo!!! I've been getting more practice speaking Chinese in public and with friends. I really enjoyed this week. I've been starting to push myself to explore more and push my social boundaries -- whenever I catch myself thinking, "Agh... it will be embarrassing/hard to speak to this worker/ask this question" it actually just reinforces my desire to do it. It's like rejection therapy. Except with being embarrassed haha.
