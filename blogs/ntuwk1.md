## Week 1: August 28th - September 3rd

## Day 1 (8/28): No Sleep Allowed
- I arrived at 4 AM but I couldn't check into my hotel until 3 PM (me four months ago thought I was saving so much money. me that morning when I arrived was feeling.... a little groggy)
- I exchanged my USD for NTD @ the airport
- 1.5 hour MRT + bus commute to my hotel! I got so lost navigating from Taipei Main Station to the bus station... I think I'm going to meet my family at the airport when they come so they don't have to go through what I did
![pic](/blog/taiwan/arrival.jpg)
- When I got on the bus, the driver asked me in English if I was American and I said "oh, yeah!" and then we started conversing. He was so friendly and extroverted; we talked about my study abroad program, Taylor Swift, Las Vegas. Thank you for the warm welcome to 台北 !
- 9 AM, I dropped off my bags at the hotel
- 10 AM, went to Chunghwa Telecom to get my 90-day eSim
- 中文 TIL: It's better to ask "你会讲英文" than "你可以说英文“ because the latter has the tone of a command
- 10:30 AM - 1 PM: Ordered a drink in English at this cafe. 
- 中文 TIL: My go-to phrasing for drinks now: 中杯，正常冰，少糖
- Worked on all my school documents since I realized I needed to do my physical check up + get my insurance plan set up for registration -- frantically looked for hospitals, asked around in NTU International student chatrooms, etc. 
![pic](/blog/taiwan/cafe.jpg)
- 1 - 3 PM: Had some curry at this really nice cafe! The waiter was so friendly and accommodating 
![pic](/blog/taiwan/curry.jpg)
- 3 PM: Dropped off my stuff at the hotel then headed to the NTU Health Clinic to get my appointment set up
- 4 PM: The view of Taipei 101 on the way back! 
![pic](/blog/taiwan/101.jpg)
- 6 PM: Knocked out

The soy milk was good but I don't really like traditional Taiwanese breakfast. It seems pretty heavy and oily. 油条is a staple and it's just fried dough sticks.
