Saturday, September 19th - Friday, September 25th

My aunt, uncle, and younger cousin visited me this week after their 3-week tour in Vietnam and it was so bittersweet. Admittedly, while they were here, I was a little disappointed. I was so excited about sharing my life in Taiwan and showing them cool sites, but they were already sick and tired from their Vietnam trip and had also "seen it all" in Vietnam.

After they left, I was really grateful and homesick, though. They added the extra leg to their trip just to visit me, and they took care of me really well while I was here by trying to pay for *everything*. And every store we walked into, my aunt asked me, "Do you need this? Or this? OR THIS??"

For the first time since arriving, I teared up a bit because I missed home so much

I've been learning more and more about the necessity of rest and, also, that improper rest can make me feel worse afterward.
If I spend 2 hours scrolling/watching videos, that's not truly rest. 
I think true rest is being able to sit without distracting your mind. I've been really enjoying drawing, reading, walking. These moments give God the chance to really speak to you and convict you.
