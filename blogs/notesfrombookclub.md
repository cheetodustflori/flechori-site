## The Pursuit of God

This past summer, I had the privilege of hosting Bethel Christian Church's annual summer book club *~~and of lowkey losing the church key~~*!

We went over *The Pursuit of God* by AW Tozer. I had gifted this book to Kay back in 2024 for Christmas, but then I borrowed it right after so I could read it myself mwahaha. I remember coming away with a positive impression... but all details escaped me.

The year prior to this, I had gone over *The Mortification of Sin* per Pastor Anson's recommendation. It was a valuable read -- but it was a tough one. The language was old, the chapters were dense, and I always felt underprepared for discussion.

This time, I wanted to choose a short book with solid teachings. That's why, by the Grace of God, we came to *The Pursuit of God.* I had a really wonderful time being part of these discussions this summer. Everyone had very unique but meaningful contributions, and I was even grateful to those who had barriers that they happily overcame to join our meetings; From Justin Huang, who zoom'ed in for several meetings and made the 1.5 hour commute from Naperville, to Yejoon, who doesn't know how to read.

This post is just a dump of important takeaways I got from each chapter and maybe a final reflection at the end if I'm feeling motivated.
