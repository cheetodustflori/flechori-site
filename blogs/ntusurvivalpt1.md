## **✅ THE MOST IMPORTANT CHECKLIST OF YOUR LIFE:**
*Note: Did this in chronological order (ish) so it could be easy to follow; dates will not be exact for future years but I wanted to provide dates that applied to my program so you could get a sense of the timeline*

**1. Get your school's admission letter!**
- I got my notification by June 14th by an "unofficial" online letter
- My school's study abroad office received an "official" physical letter around June 30th
- **Deadline: Before you get your VISA**

![offadmission.jpg](/blog/unoffletter.png)
![offadmission.jpg](/blog/offadmission.jpg)
 
**2. Apply for your VISA asap!**
- If you're doing the Fall semester like me, then you'll want a **Visitor Visa**
- Apply online at the [Visa Application Form](https://visawebapp.boca.gov.tw/BOCA_EVISA/Disclaimer?pageLocale=en_US) 
- Print out the form and BRING IT TO YOUR LOCAL CONSULATE
- I paid $185 in CASH for the Multi-Entry Visitor Visa (which means I get to exit/return to the country during my Visa but I also have to reapply prior to 15 days of my Visa expiration, which is 90 days) 
- Luckily, my consulate got it finished for me within 2 days, but it can take up to 2 WEEKS
- ❗❗Important notes: Bring CASH (the Chicago consulate didn't take personal checks or card), bring every document ever (I brought copies of my admission letters, 2 copies of 2x2 passport photos that were printed from Walgreens -- *hack*: you can take the photo at home and then print the 4 picture wallet photo set so it's cheaper than getting actual photos at Walgreens, school transcript)
- **Deadline: 7/31**; Upload to the OIA System

*Unimportant, funny side story: I didn't realize that the Visa application process required going in-person so I filled out the online form and then.... waited 2 weeks before realizing that the Visa deadline was in 1 week before RUSHINGGG to the consulate... and then they extended the deadline by 1 month el oh el. Shoutout to James for pulling through and picking up my Visa for me. u a real one.*

**3. Sign housing lease + wire deposit funds**
- I applied for the Shui Yuan Single Suite dorm back when I first applied in March and I got notified that I was assigned to it by an email on 7/8
- They required us to wire in a deposit of $990 by 7/20 to reserve our room; this amount included the $330 as the booking deposite and $660 as the security deposit
- I also had to sign the lease within *5 days* of receiving the email (so by 7/13)
- **Deadline: 7/13 and 7/20**

**4. Visit your physician + get the health forms filled out**
- NTU doesn't want to see a single *CAVITY* bro
- They are vetting students like they're enlisting them in the military
- Go to your physician IN YOUR HOME COUNTRY (to save the headaches of language barriers, insurance, scheduling appointments, etc.) and bring the medical forms below (these may change year-to-year so CHECK the correct forms)
- You can check for the most up-to-date medical exam requirements [here](https://shmc.ntu.edu.tw/en/HealthCheckup/Internationalstudents) and scroll down to **Incoming Exchange/Visiting students**
- Otherwise, you'll have to go through the process of getting these forms filled out at NTU and it will delay your registration and consequently your student ID processing
- **Deadline: 9/4**

**5. Purchase accident AND medical insurance with *separate* totals**
- Accident insurance worth a minimum of TWD 1,000,000
- Medical insurance worth a minimum of TWD 1,000,000
- My school took care of my insurance but make sure you *clarify* with your study abroad office the specific requirements because they'll often just buy a general plan for all their students without looking into the specifics
- **Deadline: BEFORE you arrive in Taiwan**

**6. Print extra copies of everything**
- 2x2 Passport photos
- Admission letter
- Passport copy

**7. Debit card**
- Had to add this because I've actually forgotten this... 
- You will need cash. Pls bring your debit card.

**8. Course Registration**
- If this isn't the prime example of NTU overcomplicating everything, idk what is
- There are TWO course registration time slots where you can put in your PREFERENCE for courses from ~ Aug 19-21 and ~ Aug 24-26 in TAIWAN time
- You'll be notified the day after if you actually got those courses
- This has to be done through TWO applications: [Course.ntu.edu](https://course.ntu.edu.tw/en) and [Course Selection Site](https://if192.aca.ntu.edu.tw/sel-info/index.html)
- **Deadline: During one of the two slots mentioned above**
