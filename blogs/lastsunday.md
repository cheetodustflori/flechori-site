This past Sunday, Jina, Ellen, and Kou left for church without me. Normally, James drives all of the college students in his van, but Jina drove her car separately and abandoned Kay and me without a word >:(

**To say I was upset was an understatement** 😡

I called Kou while I was in the van and tried to grill her, but they pretended I was cutting out 😭 What treachery!

But then at church.... I realized they wanted to surprise me with a Jellycat cake.... 🍰 AWWWWWW.teehee oopsie. Kay pulled out a poster (that looked like a funeral poster) and had everyone at church sign it for me. It was one of the most heartwarming surprises. 

Honestly, this Sunday was so incredibly bittersweet. 

I have been overwhelmed with so much love and compassion... and now I have to leave it all for an country where no one will truly know or care for me like my friends currently do. I wanted to feel more fully grateful, but I suddenly had this nagging sense of doubt. 

*Am I making the right decision to leave?* 
*I spent so long developing these precious relationships - why am I putting it on pause?* 

This post is my reflection on **sisterhood, community in faith, and the little that I've learned and more I want to learn about having a steadfast faith** through *all situations.*

## Sisterhood

Throughout college, I have developed some incredible relationships. My sisters amaze me everyday with their selflessness, humility, tenacity, desire to befriend all personalities, love for serving, and honesty. I have no doubt about God's work in how all of our paths have crossed and aligned unexpectedly.

Through these friendships, I've been confronted with some hard truths as well. 

It's easy for me to conflate my love for my friends with love for God. I can idolize praise from them and perform good acts in pursuit of validation. When conflict arises, I have a hard time setting aside emotions and recognizing my friends as God's creation.

These have been lessons that I've been constantly growing and learning from.

**But I anticipate this new lesson might be the hardest: Although my friends are dear to me, they are not a substitute for the perfect love from God.** 

Now that I'm going off to Taiwan, I want to realign my heart to remember my friendships properly. 

I don't want the physical absence of my sisters to affect my pursuit of God. I want to fully trust in His love even when I'm not receiving it in the same way from the people around me. 

Even more importantly, I want to share this love just as fervently with those around me, regardless of our closeness. 

## Biblical Relationships

The Bible has a lot to say about how we should treat those around us. 

To our enemies:
**“If you love those who love you, what benefit is that to you? For even sinners love those who love them. And if you do good to those who do good to you, what benefit is that to you? For even sinners do the same. And if you lend to those from whom you expect to receive, what credit is that to you? Even sinners lend to sinners, to get back the same amount. But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, expecting nothing in return, and your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the Most High, for he is kind to the ungrateful and the evil. Be merciful, even as your Father is merciful." Luke 6:32-36**

To those around us: 
**"Do not neglect to do good and to share what you have, for such sacrifices are pleasing to God." Hebrews 13:16**
